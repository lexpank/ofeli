\doxysubsection{Detailed Description}
The child class \doxylink{classofeli_1_1_a_cwithout_edges_y_u_v}{ACwithout\+Edges\+YUV} implements a function to calculate specifically speed {\itshape Fd} based on the Chan-\/\+Vese model, a region-\/based energy functional. The regularization of our active contour is performed by a gaussian smoothing of \doxylink{classofeli_1_1_active_contour_aacb03a6ded4ca51cb52f58aeff955ef7}{phi} so we are interested uniquely by the external or data dependant term of this energy functional.~\newline
 $F_{d}=\lambda _{out}\left[ \alpha \left( Y_{out}-C_{outC}\right) ^{2}+ \beta \left( U_{out}-C_{outU}\right) ^{2}+ \gamma \left( V_{out}-C_{outV}\right) ^{2}\right] + \lambda _{in}\left[ \alpha \left( Y_{in}-C_{inY}\right) ^{2}+ \beta \left( U_{in}-C_{inU}\right) ^{2}+ \gamma \left( V_{in}-C_{inV}\right) ^{2}\right]$
\begin{DoxyItemize}
\item $F_{d}$ :\+ data dependant evolution speed calculated for each point of the active contour, only it sign is used by the algorithm. ~\newline

\item $Y$ :\+ luminance component Y of the (Y,U,V) color space of the current pixel of the active contour. ~\newline

\item $U$ :\+ chrominance component U of the (Y,U,V) color space of the current pixel of the active contour. ~\newline

\item $V$ :\+ chrominance component V of the (Y,U,V) color space of the current pixel of the active contour. ~\newline

\item $C_{out}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels outside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) >0$. ~\newline

\item $C_{in}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels inside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) <0$. ~\newline

\item $C_{out}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels outside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) >0$. ~\newline

\item $C_{in}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels inside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) <0$. ~\newline

\item $C_{out}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels outside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) >0$. ~\newline

\item $C_{in}$ :\+ mean of the intensities or grey-\/levels of the pixels inside the curve, i.\+e. pixels $i$ with $\phi \left( i\right) <0$. ~\newline

\item $\lambda _{out}$ :\+ weight of the outside homogeneity criterion in the Chan-\/\+Vese model. ~\newline

\item $\lambda _{in}$ :\+ weight of the inside homogeneity criterion in the Chan-\/\+Vese model. ~\newline

\item $\alpha$ :\+ weight of the luminance component Y. ~\newline

\item $\gamma$ :\+ weight of the chrominance component V.
